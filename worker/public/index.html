How it works

Three steps. No wristband.

  1. 01

    Set it up

    Name the doors or areas you control, then the groups or ticket types that can enter each one. Generate invites, or add people by email.

  2. 02

    Add the invite

    Sign in with a phone number or email, add the code once, and the pass lives on your phone. Each code works one time only.

  3. 03

    Show it, get in

    Staff scan your pass and see admit or do not admit, your first name and your group. Every scan is logged. At the till, pay the same way.

    Built for trust.

    Invites that cannot be reused

    Codes are checked and used up in a single step, so two people cannot share one invite or take the last ticket.

    Codes never stored in the clear

    Only a scrambled version is kept. Even someone who read the database could not turn it back into working invites or keys.

    Privacy at the door

    Staff see a first name and last initial, not your phone number or email. A till never sees your balance.
