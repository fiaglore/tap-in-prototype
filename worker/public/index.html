For guests

Your pass, always in your pocket.

Get in

One ticket, one person

Buy on the event’s page or add an invite code, sign in with your phone number or email, and the pass lives in the Tap In app.

Stay safe

A code that keeps moving

With Live QR the code on your phone changes every 30 seconds, so a screenshot sent to someone else stops working.

Walk in

Show it and go

Staff scan your phone and get a clear yes or no. No printout, no wristband queue, and no signal needed for live passes.

How it works

Three steps to the door.

  1. 01

    Find your event

    Browse what is coming up, or open the invite link or code you were sent.

  2. 02

    Get your pass

    Sign in with a phone number or email. Your ticket appears in the app and belongs to you.

  3. 03

    Tap in

    Show your pass at the gate. Staff see Admit or Do not admit, and you are through.

Beyond the ticket sale

Invitations, weddings and offices too.

Weddings and private parties

Invitations that check you in

Your host sends a link on WhatsApp. Say whether you are coming and how many, with no app or account. At the door, one scan lets your whole party in.

Tickets from elsewhere

Bring the ticket you bought

If the organiser imported the buyer list, your ticket works at the gate once, and copies are turned away.

Your privacy

Staff see a first name

Gate staff see your first name and last initial, never your phone number or email.
