Entry without bands

Your phone is the pass.

Organisers send an invite code. You add it once. At the door you show your phone and walk in. No wristbands to print, hand out, lose or throw away.

How it works

1 · Organiser

Create the event

Name the areas you control (main gate, VIP lounge, backstage) and the ticket types that can enter each one. Generate invite codes in bulk, or tie a code to one person.

2 · Attendee

Add the invite

Sign in with your phone number or email, type the code once, and the pass lives in your phone. Each code works one time only.

3 · Gate

Show it, get in

Gate staff scan the QR on your pass. They see a clear admit or do not admit, your first name, and your ticket type. Every scan is logged live.

Built for trust

Invites that cannot be reused

Codes are checked and used up in a single step, so two people cannot share one invite.

Codes never stored in the clear

Only a scrambled version is kept. Even someone who read the database could not turn it back into working invites.

Privacy at the door

Staff see a first name and last initial, not your phone number or email.

Tap In is a work in progress. Sign-in codes are shown on screen in demo mode until a text or email provider is connected.
